Company Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused annual leave up to 5 days may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Employees must apply for annual leave at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year and is not encashable.

3. Casual Leave
Employees may take up to 6 days of casual leave per calendar year for personal reasons. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave for more than 3 consecutive days without manager approval.

4. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 10 days of paid paternity leave, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

5. Work From Home Policy
Employees may request up to 8 work-from-home days per month, subject to manager approval. Work-from-home requests should be submitted at least 1 day in advance.

6. Leave Encashment
At the end of employment, employees may encash a maximum of 15 unused annual leave days. Sick leave and casual leave are not eligible for encashment.

7. Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year as per the official holiday calendar published in January.
